A hen party nobody will forget

The whole catamaran for your group: a captain, a decorated deck, a photo zone on the bow and the open sea instead of a club terrace.

Discuss the dates

Why a catamaran beats a club

A club is one evening and a dozen identical photos. A catamaran is whole days that belong only to your group: your friends, the sea, the music and not a single stranger nearby. The boat is yours — nobody sits down at the next table and nobody decides when the terrace closes.

The format works for a single day (out of the marina in the morning, back by evening), for a weekend, or for a full week at sea. We organise all of it — from the boat and the route to decorating the deck and booking the photographer.

How the day goes

  • We meet you at the marina — champagne and photos aboard before casting off;
  • a hop to a quiet bay: swimming in open water, not in a hotel pool;
  • lunch and snacks cooked on board;
  • SUP, snorkelling, music, a photo session on the bow and the trampolines;
  • sunset on deck — the most photographed part of the day;
  • back to the marina, or a night at anchor if the format runs longer.

What we take care of

  • A catamaran for your dates — catamarans only, never monohulls; broker access to 260+ charter companies worldwide;
  • a captain on board, so nobody in the group has to stay responsible;
  • deck decoration and a photo zone on the bow, styled to your theme;
  • provisioning, drinks, music and the shape of the day;
  • a chef and a photographer on request;
  • transfers from the airport or hotel to the marina.

Formats and budget

A mini series catamaran for 6–8 people — from €8,800 per week; premium catamarans from €10,800 (8–10 people) and from €12,500 (8–12 people). Short formats (a day, a weekend, 2–3 days) are quoted individually. For groups over 12 we put together a flotilla.

Where we sail

Most often Turkey (Fethiye and Gocek — a short flight and gentle seas) and Greece. In winter, Thailand and the Caribbean; for a special occasion, the Seychelles. We also do weddings aboard, from chartering the boat to a ceremony at sunset by an island.

How to start

Send us the number of guests, your preferred dates and the format (a day, a weekend, a week) — we will come back with two or three catamarans and an exact price. Book early: good boats on popular dates go months ahead.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a hen party on a yacht cost?

A mini series catamaran for 6-8 people starts at EUR 8,800 per week; premium catamarans are from EUR 10,800 and EUR 12,500. One-day and two-to-three-day formats are quoted individually. Tell us the dates and the number of guests and we will name the exact figure.

How many people fit aboard?

The mini series catamaran takes 6-8 guests, premium catamarans take 8-10 and 8-12, and every cabin has its own head. A day trip can carry more. For larger groups we put together a flotilla.

Can we do a single day without staying overnight?

Yes. Out of the marina in the morning, back in the evening — the most common hen party format. Staying aboard overnight is optional.

Do we need experience, and will we get seasick?

No experience needed — the captain runs the boat. A catamaran sits on two hulls and barely heels, and on a celebration day we plan the route through sheltered water.

Can we decorate the boat and bring our own props?

Yes. We build a photo zone on the bow and dress the deck to your theme, and you are welcome to bring your own decor — send us the list and we will have it set up before you arrive.

Can we bring alcohol and play music?

Both, yes. We handle the provisioning and there are speakers aboard. The only limit is quiet hours at night in the marina — everything before that is yours.
