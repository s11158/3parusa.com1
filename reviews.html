What our guests say

Nine stories from people who spent a week with us aboard a catamaran. Real names and photos; the texts are translated from Russian and lightly condensed.

We do not buy reviews and never ask for five stars. These are the words guests sent us themselves after the trip.

Pavel Sapozhnikov

3Parusa guest · catamaran tour · translated from Russian

In one year I visited several new countries and regions, and even the familiar Egypt and Turkey opened up from a completely new side. Every trip is a chain of insights: Turkey gave me sailing. Nothing bonds people like travelling together and the emotions you live through as a crew. Every tour is accompanied by Nikita, a hugely talented photographer, so I already have hundreds of stunning photos.

Marina Prikhodko

3Parusa guest · catamaran tour · translated from Russian

I cannot help sharing the feelings I had on the regatta with Travel Business Club and 3Parusa Yacht Travel. It was my first time, so I worried about seasickness, but it turned out far easier than I feared. Our captain Stas ran the briefing and two catamarans set off. Every day brought stunning sunrises, lagoons and unbelievable sunsets. The greatest treasure were the people I spent seven days at sea with. This is the trip where I found my people and the man I love.

Tatiana Starichikhina

3Parusa guest · catamaran tour · translated from Russian

I love everything about the sea, and this was the case where reality exceeded every expectation. The cabins are like hotel rooms, just more compact, with a private bathroom for each one. The galley has fridges, a stove and all the cookware you need. Then seven days of pure bliss: towns and bays of unreal beauty, dinners in local restaurants and cooked by ourselves, outdoors 24/7 in swimsuits and barefoot. For the first time in my life I switched my mind off completely. Thank you to our captains Stas Kochukov and Evgeny Gulin for their professionalism.

Svetlana Pimenova

3Parusa guest · catamaran tour · translated from Russian
