Seychelles yacht charter: a week on a skippered catamaran

3Parusa → Destinations → Seychelles Yacht Charter

A week aboard a catamaran across an archipelago of 115 islands: Mahe, Praslin, La Digue and the small islands the ferries never reach. Captain, curated route, 6 to 12 guests. A cabin starts at €1,500 per person, the whole catamaran at €8,800 per week. Season: November to April.

This is not a day boat rental and not a cruise ship. You get the catamaran to yourself, your own route and a captain who sails the Seychelles every season and knows these boats and anchorages personally. Our team handles everything: the boat, the moorings, transfers and the shape of each day. It works for groups of friends, families with children and corporate teams alike.

The week's route

Eden (Mahe) → Sainte Anne → Praslin → Aride → La Digue → Grande Soeur → Silhouette → Victoria → Mahe Marina

Hops between islands are short — usually 1 to 3 hours in sheltered water — so a day aboard is not travelling, it is a series of stops. A new anchorage every evening, a new island every morning. The route is not fixed: we adjust the order of islands to the wind and to you.

  • Mahe — rainforest and national parks, the start and finish of the trip.
  • Sainte Anne — a marine park next to the capital: snorkelling from day one.
  • Praslin — the Vallee de Mai reserve, home of the legendary coco de mer.
  • Aride, Grande Soeur, Silhouette — small islands, giant tortoises a century old, empty beaches.
  • La Digue — the granite boulders from every postcard, bicycles instead of cars.
  • Victoria — the smallest capital in the world, a market of spices and vanilla.

The beaches here look like a commercial and stay empty, because you arrive from the sea, where tour buses do not go. On request we add exotic fishing and — for the brave — a night beside an uninhabited island.

The Seychelles are a premium destination: the fleet is small and pricier than the Mediterranean, so a week here costs more than Turkey or Greece. We sail catamarans only and keep three lines: a mini series for 6 to 8 guests and two premium series for 8 to 10 and 8 to 12. The exact figure follows once the dates and the boat are set.

What is already included

Accommodation aboard a VIP catamaran (double cabins, each with its own head and shower), a curated route, the captain's work, linen and towels, Wi-Fi, airport-to-marina transfers and 24/7 support.

What is paid separately

Flights (we will help you pick them) and the boat kitty — a shared pot for provisions, fuel and moorings; we quote it for the specific route and show the receipts at the end of the trip. On request: a chef and a photographer aboard, SUP and gear rental. The yacht deposit is refundable, and it is often smarter to take full insurance instead of freezing the money — we will advise which works better in your case.

Season: when to go

There is no winter in the Seychelles: air and water hold around 28 °C all year. The trade winds blow from the east for half the year and from the west for the other half, so sailing is possible any month. The most comfortable window is November to April — no rainy season, steady wind, calm hops between islands.

Our 3Parusa group departures fall in November and April.

The catamaran and the cabins

We do not offer monohulls as a matter of principle — catamarans only, for stability and space. That means double cabins with private heads, a large saloon, a shaded deck and trampolines on the bow, where guests end up spending half the trip. A catamaran sits on two hulls and barely heels, so motion is hardly noticeable — which matters if you sail with children or worry about seasickness.

You can take the whole boat for your own group, or book one or two cabins on a shared departure — in which case we match the group on board ourselves.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Seychelles yacht charter cost?

A cabin on a shared departure starts at €1,500 per person per week. The whole catamaran starts at €8,800 per week for our mini series (6–8 guests); premium catamarans are from €10,800 (8–10 guests) and from €12,500 (8–12 guests). Flights and the boat kitty — the shared pot for food, fuel and moorings — are paid separately; we show the receipts at the end of the trip.

When is the best season to sail the Seychelles?

You can sail year-round: air and water stay around 28 °C. The most comfortable window is November to April — no rainy season, steady wind and calm hops between islands. Our group departures usually fall in November and April.

Do I need a visa for the Seychelles?

No visa is required — entry permission is issued on arrival. You need a passport, a return ticket and proof of accommodation; we provide the yacht confirmation ourselves.

Do I need sailing experience?

No. Our captain runs the boat, you are a guest. If you want to take the helm and learn the ropes, we will teach you — but only if you feel like it.

How many people fit on the catamaran?

It depends on the series: the mini catamaran takes 6–8 guests, premium catamarans take 8–10 and 8–12. Every cabin has its own head with a shower. For groups over 12 we put together a flotilla.

Can I book a single cabin instead of the whole boat?

Yes. On shared departures we sell individual cabins and match the group on board ourselves. A cabin in the Seychelles starts at €1,500 per person; the exact price depends on the boat and the dates.

How far in advance should I book?

Six months to a year. The Seychelles fleet is small — there are only a handful of good large catamarans and European charterers take them early. New Year dates and April go first.

Does a catamaran make you seasick?

A catamaran sits on two hulls and barely heels, and the hops between Seychelles islands are short — usually 1–3 hours in sheltered water. Most guests never notice any motion.
