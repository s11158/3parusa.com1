Why a catamaran

A club is the tired classic: loud, expensive and nothing to tell afterwards. A catamaran entirely at your disposal is a story the group retells for years. No neighbours, no door policy, no bill for the table — just you, the sea and a captain who solves every problem.

We run single-day trips, weekends and full weeks. Organisation is entirely on us: boat, route, provisioning, program.

What it looks like

  • The whole boat is yours: decks, saloon, trampolines on the bow, the cockpit;
  • swimming in open water and bays that day trips never reach;
  • food cooked aboard, with a chef on request;
  • the program follows the mood: a regatta challenge between crews, or a quiet bay and shisha in the cockpit;
  • fishing, SUP, snorkelling, wakeboarding — by arrangement;
  • a night at anchor under the stars, or back to the marina.

What we take care of

  • A catamaran for your dates — catamarans only, never monohulls; broker access to 260+ charter companies worldwide;
  • a captain, so nobody in the group has to stay sober and responsible;
  • provisioning, music and the program;
  • a chef and a photographer on request;
  • airport-to-marina transfers and 24/7 support.

Formats and budget

A mini series catamaran for 6–8 people — from €8,800 per week; premium catamarans from €10,800 (8–10 people) and from €12,500 (8–12 people). Short formats (a day, a weekend, 2–3 days) are quoted individually. For groups over 12 we put together a flotilla.

Where we sail

Mainly Turkey (Fethiye, Gocek), Greece, Croatia and Ibiza. In winter, Thailand and the Caribbean. If it is a company team rather than friends, see corporate retreats.

How to start

Send us the number of guests, the dates and the format — we will come back with boats and an exact price. In high season the good catamarans go months ahead, so the earlier you ask, the wider the choice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a stag party on a yacht cost?

A mini series catamaran for 6-8 people starts at EUR 8,800 per week; premium catamarans are from EUR 10,800 and EUR 12,500. One-day and two-to-three-day formats are quoted individually.

How many people can we bring?

The mini series catamaran sleeps 6-8, premium catamarans 8-10 and 8-12. A day trip can carry more. For groups over 12 we put together a flotilla of catamarans.

Can we go for a weekend or a single day?

Yes. A day trip and a 2-3 day weekend are the most common formats. A full week makes sense if you want a real route with island hops.

Do we need sailing experience?

No, our captain is aboard. If you want to steer and hoist the sails we will show you, but only if you feel like it.

Can we be loud, with music and drinks?

At sea, yes — the boat is yours. Marinas have quiet hours at night, so we plan the loud part of the evening for an anchorage in a bay.

What if someone is afraid of seasickness?

That is exactly why we sail catamarans: two hulls, minimal heel, wide decks. We plan the route through sheltered water and most guests never notice any motion.
